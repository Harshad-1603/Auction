The Source Auction

Bid smart. React to new evidence. Negotiate. Then choose and rank the six sources you trust most.

🎤 Host-controlled 💰 $1,000 / group 🔟 10 auction lots 🤝 Trading round 🏆 Top-6 ranking
1

Bid

Each group starts with $1,000. The host shows one mystery source at a time. Groups bid verbally.

2

Discover

You will not know everything when you buy. More source intelligence is revealed only after the auction.

3

Negotiate

After the reveal, groups get a short chance to buy, sell or swap sources with each other.

4

Rank

Each group chooses only its Top 6 sources from A–J and ranks those six from strongest to weakest.

How do you win?

Build a strong research portfolio, avoid wasting your budget, and make a strong final Top-6 judgement. The exact model ranking and scoring weights stay hidden until the results screen.
